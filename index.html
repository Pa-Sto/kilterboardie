Pick grade, angle, and model. Both generators predict the 4 route channels while conditioning on hold presence, size, and orientation features.

Dataset and Model Details

Dataset exports live as 34 x 35 x 10 tensors. Dynamic channels are start, finish, hand, and foot. Static conditioning channels are hold_presence, hold_size, orient_sin1, orient_cos1, orient_sin2, and orient_cos2.

The CVAE is a compact grade-conditioned latent model. The diffusion model is a grade- and timestep-conditioned U-Net trained with denoising, reconstruction, count, path, upward, and density losses for more structured samples.
